Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our trading challenges, rules, and payout process.

What is the maximum drawdown rule?+
Our maximum drawdown rule specifies the maximum percentage your account balance or equity can drop from the initial balance. For our Standard Challenge, it is 10%. For example, on a $10,000 account, your equity cannot fall below $9,000 at any point.
What is the profit target to pass the challenge?+
To pass the UTABOON Challenge, you need to achieve a specific profit target, which varies by account type. For the Standard Challenge, the profit target is 8% of the initial account balance. For a $10,000 account, this means reaching $800 in profits.
How and when do I receive payouts?+
Payouts are processed swiftly upon request, typically within 24-48 hours. You can request a payout after 14 days of trading on your funded account. We support various methods including bank transfer and crypto, ensuring convenience.
What trading instruments are allowed?+
You can trade a wide range of instruments including Forex pairs, indices, commodities, and cryptocurrencies. We aim to provide flexibility for diverse trading strategies. Specific details are available in your trader dashboard.
What happens if I breach a rule?+
Breaching a rule, such as exceeding the maximum daily loss or overall drawdown, will result in the termination of your challenge or funded account. We offer options to retry the challenge at a discounted rate, acknowledging that mistakes happen.
Is the challenge fee refunded when I pass?+
Yes, for most of our challenge accounts, the initial challenge fee is refunded to you with your first profit split once you successfully pass the challenge and receive a funded account. Please check specific challenge terms for details.
Can I trade news events and hold positions overnight?+
Yes, UTABOON permits trading during news events and holding positions overnight, and over weekends. We believe in empowering skilled traders with the flexibility they need. No restrictions on strategy.
Is there a scaling plan for funded accounts?+
Absolutely! We offer an attractive scaling plan. Consistently profitable traders can increase their funded capital up to $2,000,000 based on their performance and adherence to our trading objectives.
What trading platforms are supported?+
UTABOON supports industry-leading trading platforms such as MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5. This ensures you have access to robust tools and a familiar trading environment for your convenience.
How long do I have to complete the challenge?+
Unlike many firms, UTABOON does not impose a time limit for completing our challenge. We want you to trade without pressure, focusing on strategy and discipline. Take the time you need to succeed.
