About UTABOON: Empowering Traders

We believe in true trading talent. UTABOON was founded to bridge the gap between skilled traders and capital, fostering a community of successful, independent professionals.

Our Story & Mission

UTABOON was born from a simple idea: skilled traders shouldn't be limited by their personal capital. Our founder, a long-time trader themselves, recognized the vast pool of untapped talent in the retail trading space. The mission became clear: create a transparent, fair, and supportive environment where talent is rewarded with real funding.

We built UTABOON to democratize access to capital. Traditional finance often blocks independent traders from significant opportunities. We provide the platform, the capital, and the framework for disciplined traders to truly thrive, offering profit splits that genuinely reward performance.

Today, UTABOON is a thriving community of traders who are proving that consistency and strong risk management lead to success. We're committed to continuous innovation, ensuring our traders have the best tools, support, and opportunities to reach their financial goals.
