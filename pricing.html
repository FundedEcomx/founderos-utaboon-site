Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Is there a free trial?+
Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial so you can experience the power of UTABOON for yourself. No credit card required to start your trial.
What happens after the trial ends?+
After your 7-day trial, you will be prompted to choose a paid plan to continue accessing UTABOON's features. We will notify you before your trial expires.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Absolutely. You can cancel your UTABOON subscription at any time directly from your account settings. There are no long-term contracts or hidden fees.
What integrations do you support?+
Currently, UTABOON integrates with major wearable devices and popular fitness trackers to sync your activity data seamlessly. More integrations are planned.
Is my data secure?+
Yes, your data security is our top priority. We use industry-standard encryption and robust security protocols to protect all your personal and fitness information.
Do you offer team/agency pricing?+
UTABOON is designed for solo consumers seeking personal fitness transformation. We do not currently offer specific team or agency pricing.
How long does setup take?+
Getting started with UTABOON is quick and easy. Most users can complete their initial profile setup and generate their first plan in under 10 minutes.
What support is included?+
All UTABOON plans include email support. Our dedicated team is ready to assist you with any questions or technical issues you may encounter. Elite plans include additional consultation.
