Common Questions About UTABOON Features

How does AI personalize my workouts?+
Our AI analyzes your performance, feedback, and progress over time to adjust intensity, volume, and exercise selection, ensuring optimal challenge and growth.
Can I customize my meal plans?+
Yes, UTABOON allows you to set dietary restrictions, preferences, and even swap out specific meals or ingredients to perfectly match your taste and needs.
What kind of tracking is available?+
You can track weight, body measurements, strength metrics, daily calorie intake, water consumption, and more through intuitive dashboards and logs.
Is there support for beginners?+
Absolutely! UTABOON is designed for all fitness levels. Our AI scales plans for beginners, and our extensive video library helps with proper form.
How often are plans updated?+
Your workout and meal plans are dynamically updated based on your progress and any changes you make to your goals or preferences, typically on a weekly basis.
Can I connect my fitness tracker?+
Yes, UTABOON connects with most popular fitness trackers and smartwatches to automatically sync your activity data for a holistic view of your health.
What if I have an injury?+
While UTABOON is not a substitute for medical advice, you can adjust your workouts to avoid certain exercises or muscle groups if you're recovering from an injury. Consult a doctor first.
Does UTABOON help with motivation?+
Yes, with progress tracking, achievement badges, and regular motivational insights, UTABOON keeps you engaged and inspired on your fitness journey.
