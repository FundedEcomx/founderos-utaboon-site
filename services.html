Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?
A typical project can take anywhere from a few weeks to several months, depending on the scope and complexity of the project.

We offer both retainers and project-based pricing, depending on the needs and goals of our clients.

The discovery call includes a 30-minute consultation with one of our experts to discuss your goals and objectives.
Who will be working on my project?
Our team of experts will be working on your project, including health & fitness coaches, fitness studio owners, and health & wellness center owners.

We need you to provide us with some basic information about your business, including your goals and objectives, and any relevant data or metrics.
Do you work with early-stage or bootstrapped businesses?
Yes, we work with early-stage and bootstrapped businesses, and offer customized solutions to meet their unique needs and goals.
What results can I realistically expect?
You can expect to see significant improvements in your health and fitness, as well as increased revenue and growth for your business.
How do I know if we're a good fit?
We offer a free discovery call to discuss your goals and objectives, and determine if we're a good fit for your business.
