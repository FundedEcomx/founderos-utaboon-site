Are there recurring fees for funded accounts?+
No, once you pass the challenge and receive a funded account, there are no recurring subscription fees. Your challenge fee is a one-time payment, and it's even refundable upon your first profit split.

We offer 24/7 customer support via email and live chat. Additionally, funded traders gain access to a dedicated trader success team for performance guidance and account management assistance.

Yes, absolutely. UTABOON encourages independent trading and does not impose restrictions on your trading style or strategy, provided you adhere to our risk management rules like maximum drawdown and daily loss limits.
How do I progress to a larger funded account?+
Our scaling plan allows you to grow your capital. By consistently hitting profit targets on your funded account while maintaining excellent risk management, you'll be eligible for account size increases.
